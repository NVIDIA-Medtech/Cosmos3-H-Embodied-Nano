# Reported portability checks

This is an anonymized summary of an independent review on 2026-11-04. It contains
no machine names, account names or private filesystem paths. Results below are
reported observations, not a new independent rerun by the tutorial maintainer.

| Configuration | Result |
|---|---|
| Two 48 GB GPUs: one Ampere, one Ada; display workload on the Ampere GPU | Setup, 31 tests, migration and C24/C72 H16 real-loader preflight passed |
| Host | 125 GiB RAM, 61 GiB swap, 768 GiB free disk; Linux; driver 580.126.20; Torch 2.10.0+cu130 |
| Unchanged C24 A training | OOM during first-step backward recomputation, before optimizer state allocation |
| Allocator retry | Same failure with expandable segments; approximately 41–42 GiB allocated on the failing rank |
| Released SF4/SF2 inference on the Ada GPU | Completed recorded/translation-hold/rotation-hold/energy-off evaluations; reported peak approximately 38.5 GB |
| Timing | One 73-frame rollout: approximately 45 seconds including model load; not steady-state throughput |

Both released students produced 73 frames at 832×480 and 10 Hz on three windows
from one test episode, including two windows with genuine H16 and one with H0.
The same-seed translation/rotation interventions changed output pixels. That
alone is not proof of correct action following. Energy-off was a no-op because
recorded energy was zero in every selected window.

| Window | History | SF4 PSNR | SF2 PSNR | Repeat-first PSNR | SF4 LPIPS | SF2 LPIPS |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 1 | 0 | 16.77 | 16.49 | 14.27 | 0.326 | 0.323 |
| 2 | 16 | 17.04 | 16.16 | 16.15 | 0.291 | 0.332 |
| 3 | 16 | 17.80 | 17.39 | 16.42 | 0.281 | 0.286 |

SF4 had higher PSNR in all three windows, but not better values for every metric:
SF2 had slightly lower LPIPS in the first window. Both students exceeded the
repeat-first PSNR baseline, narrowly for SF2 in window 2. These are three windows
from one episode that may overlap pretraining, not a general quality comparison.

Two deviations limit the literal tutorial qualification: model files were
prefetched to the expected paths and later checked by the helper; inference
configs were generated by separate scripts because the earlier tutorial required
configs from completed training. The new config-generation and inference entry
points replace that external step, but require their own independent GPU rerun.

The approximately 15.17B total / 6.97B trained parameter counts came from the
checkpoint header and training log. Training-state and disk totals in the tutorial
are estimates; this workstation did not write training checkpoints or run exports.
Do not infer a universal two-GPU limit, a minimum VRAM requirement for other
devices, full-curriculum convergence, smoke-export H16 qualification or meaningful
energy responsiveness from this report.
